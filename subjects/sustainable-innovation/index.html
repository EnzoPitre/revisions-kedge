KEDGE Revisions E

Sustainable Innovation

5 fiches · 1 exercice

Eco-design, design thinking, value chain, business models, sustainability culture, leadership and impact assessment (SROI).

Session 1

Fiche

Concept of Sustainable Innovation (Circular, Frugal, Social)

16 min · Definition of sustainable innovation, classic innovation (Schumpeter, incremental vs radical, value creation/capture), and the three forms: circular, frugal and social innovation.

Session 2

Fiche

Eco-design & Design Thinking

14 min · Definition of eco-design, standard design thinking and its limits, ecodesign dilemmas (Prendeville), responsible design thinking (Baldassarre) and Brown's design thinking.

Fiche

Value Chain & Sustainable Business Models

8 min · Strategic alignment for sustainability, Porter's value chain (Apple example), frugal innovation case, Business Model Canvas and the four organisational strategies of Acosta et al. (2014).

Session 3

Fiche

Sustainability Culture & Leadership

13 min · Organizational culture (Schein), sustainability-oriented culture, greenwashing, 8 drivers of transformation, leadership definitions, inspirational leadership and authentic leadership (Rehman & Zeb 2023).

Session 4

Fiche

Assessing Impact & SROI

11 min · Output vs outcome vs impact, 5 dimensions of impact, why evaluate, qualitative approach and the 6 steps of Social Return on Investment (deadweight, attribution, displacement, drop-off, NPV, ratio).

Exercices

Assessing Impact & SROI — Exercises

4 min · Re-doing the course SROI case step by step, plus generated practice on output/outcome/impact, deductions and SROI ratio.
