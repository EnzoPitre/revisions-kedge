Sociology of Social Transformations & Management

4 fiches

Why the humanities matter for managers: AI vs human judgment, sociology (holism vs individualism) and anthropology (myth, rites, doctrine).

Session 1

Fiche

AI vs Human Judgment & Introduction to Sociology

13 min · Why the humanities matter in the age of AI, the founders of sociology, and the two opposing schools (holism vs individualism).

Session 2

Fiche

Anthropology — Myth, Rites and Doctrine

15 min · Culture, symbols and meaning (Tylor, Lévi-Strauss, Cassirer, Geertz, Durand, Durkheim, Mauss, Étienne), the triptych myth/rites/doctrine and its transposition to organizations (Schein, Deal & Kennedy).

Session 3

Fiche

Economics — The Major Schools of Thought

13 min · No neutral definition of economics, the five schools (classical, Marxist, neoclassical, Keynesian, critiques), consequences for management (incentives, game theory, shareholder value) and the meta-lesson.

Session 4

Fiche

Philosophy — Traditions & Worldviews

13 min · Philosophy as questioning foundations; four domains; the great traditions (ancient, rationalism, empiricism, Enlightenment, existentialism, postmodern); Habermas, ethics lenses, Levinas, Arendt; the philosophical compass.
