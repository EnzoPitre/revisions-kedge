Exercices du cours (Excel)

Statements are those of the professor’s spreadsheet. Answers marked (corrigé du fichier) are shown in the document; the others are blank in the document and were calculées here with the course method.

Exercice 1 — PV

A legal settlement states that you have to pay $10,000 in 2 years. If the rate of return is 2%, how much money do you need to invest today to get that amount?

Voir la correction

Given: FV = 10,000 ; i = 2% ; n = 2. Formula: \(PV = FV/(1+i)^n\).

\(PV = 10{,}000 / 1.02^2 = 9{,}611.69\) (corrigé du fichier). Excel: VA.

Exercice 2 — FV

You have $12,000 to invest at 9% (US stock market) for 30 years. How much at the end?

Voir la correction

Given: PV = 12,000 ; i = 9% ; n = 30. Formula: \(FV = PV(1+i)^n\).

\(FV = 12{,}000 \times 1.09^{30} = 159{,}212.14\) (corrigé du fichier). Excel: VC.

Exercice 3 — APR

Your credit card charges a daily interest rate of 0.05%. What is the APR?

Voir la correction

APR = periodic rate × periods per year = 0.05% × 365 = 18.25% (value also given in the lecture).

Exercice 4 — EAR

Same card (daily rate 0.05% in the statement). What is the EAR?

Voir la correction

Formula: \(EAR = (1+\text{periodic})^{365} - 1\).

\(1.0005^{365} - 1 \approx 0.2002\) → ≈ 20.02% (calculé).

Attention

The spreadsheet cell shows a periodic rate of 0.1% (not 0.05%). With 0.1%: \(1.001^{365}-1 \approx 44.03\%\). The two documents are inconsistent; check with the professor which one is expected.

Exercice 5 — PVA

You pay €600 rent per month for the next 2 years. Your parents put enough money in a bank account (2% per year) to pay exactly the rent. How much did they give you?

Voir la correction

Given: a = 600 ; n = 2 × 12 = 24 ; periodic rate = 2%/12 (assumed: the statement says “2% per year”, and the other exercises use APR/12).

PVA = sum of the PV of the 24 payments (Excel VA) ≈ €14,104.29 (calculé).

Exercice 6 — FVA

A landlord receives €100 rent per month for 3 years and reinvests at 2% per year. How much after 3 years?

Voir la correction

Given: a = 100 ; n = 36 ; periodic rate = 2%/12 (same assumption).

FVA (Excel VC) ≈ €3,707.01 (calculé).

Exercice 7 — PMT from a loan

You borrow €10,000 for 5 years at an APR of 5%. Monthly payment?

Voir la correction

PMT ≈ €188.71 (calculé).

Exercice 8 — PMT to reach a future amount

Your uncle will give you €30,000 in 4 years. You ask for monthly payments instead. How much per month if his bank pays an APR of 2%?

Voir la correction

Exercices d’entraînement

Exercice d’entraînement généré

A — Present value

You owe €25,000 in 3 years. The rate of return is 4%. How much must you invest today?

Voir la correction

1. Données : FV = 25,000 ; i = 4% ; n = 3.

2. Formule : \(PV = FV/(1+i)^n\).

3. Remplacement : \(PV = 25{,}000/1.04^3\).

4. Calcul : \(1.04^3 = 1.124864\) → \(25{,}000/1.124864 \approx 22{,}224.91\).

5. Résultat : €22,224.91. Si tu trouves 25,000/1.04 ou 25,000/1.12 : l’exposant n’est pas appliqué correctement.

Exercice d’entraînement généré

B — Future value of an annuity

You save €150 a month for 5 years in an account paying an APR of 3%. How much do you have at the end?

Voir la correction

1. Données : a = 150 ; periodic rate = 3%/12 = 0.25% ; n = 5 × 12 = 60.

2. Méthode : FVA (Excel VC) = somme des FV de chaque versement.

3–4. Calcul (Excel) : FVA ≈ 9,697.01.

5. Résultat : ≈ €9,697.01 (versements totaux : 150 × 60 = 9,000 ; le reste, ≈ 697, vient des intérêts).

Exercice d’entraînement généré

C — APR and EAR

A loan charges 1.5% per month. Find the APR and the EAR.

Voir la correction

1. Données : periodic rate = 1.5% ; m = 12.

2. APR = periodic × m = 1.5% × 12 = 18%.

3. EAR = \((1.015)^{12} - 1\) ; \(1.015^{12} \approx 1.19562\).

4. Résultat : EAR ≈ 19.56%.

5. Interprétation : le taux réellement payé par an (19.56%) est supérieur à l’APR (18%).
